# The model is not advice

It will not tell you what to do about a specific person tonight.

It will tell you what variable you're actually uncertain about.

---

## Two questions, same situation

- "Do they like me?" — first-person, no stable answer
- "What's the current information asymmetry between these two parties?" —
  systems question, has a method

This course only asks the second kind.

---

## Why finance, why game theory

- attraction, dependency, and commitment are treated as **priced positions**,
  not feelings to be resolved
- the same tools that price risk under incomplete information work here,
  because the uncertainty is the same kind
- the payoff: an anxiety-inducing, reactive domain becomes something you can
  actually plan against

## What a case review is

- bring a real or composite situation — never someone's private details
  without their consent
- leave with a **clearer statement** of the situation, not a verdict
- the model gets applied to the same case, week over week, all semester

---

## What a case review is not

- it is not couples counselling
- it is not a prediction service
- it does not replace judgement — it gives you cheaper variables to apply
  judgement to

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> A relationship is a two-party system. Each party holds information the
> other is pricing in. Everything else this semester is detail.

---

## Before session 1

- pick a situation: yours, a friend's (with care), or a plausible composite
- write it in two sentences, without naming anyone
- state what "success" would look like in terms a stranger could check
