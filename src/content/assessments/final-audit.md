## The brief

> Apply the full model — all ten variables — to one case, and show that the
> reading composes into a single coherent account.

This is where the semester's claim gets tested directly: not that any one
variable is insightful on its own, but that the ten compose into something
more useful than a folk-vocabulary read of the same case would give you.
The case can be the one you've carried since week 1, or a different one if
that case has resolved or lost momentum — either way, it needs enough
history behind it for all ten variables to have something to say.

Realistic scope: this is a single case read carefully, not a comparative
study. A final audit that tries to cover three cases in the space meant for
one case's full model application is the most common way this assignment
overruns its brief.

## What you submit

A single PDF, 2000–2800 words, working through the ten variables in the
order the course introduced them, plus a closing stop-loss condition and
Black Swan Protocol step for the case. Because this is holistically marked,
there's no per-criterion breakdown to hit — a reading that's thin on one
variable but exceptionally clear on how the others interact can still mark
well, provided the omission is acknowledged rather than silently skipped.
