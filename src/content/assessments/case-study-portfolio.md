## The brief

> Compile three cases, at least one of them stalled, and classify each
> against reciprocity, the commitment gate, and contract type.

Where the position paper asked for depth on one case, this portfolio asks
for range across three. At least one case should be a genuinely stalled
one, classified against one of the three named situationship failure modes
(asymmetric-cost, sunk-specificity, silent-substitute) — a portfolio of
three clean, uncomplicated cases doesn't exercise the part of the model this
assignment is checking.

The mechanics: three separate write-ups, each independently classified. A
case that closed cleanly into a named contract type (Date, Exclusive,
Official, Investment) is a valid inclusion and doesn't need to be stalled to
count — the requirement is that at least one of the three is.

## What you submit

A single PDF containing three case studies, 500–700 words each. Analytical
depth (50%) is marked per case on whether the classification holds up under
the evidence presented. Use of the model's terminology (30%) checks that
"attachment," "dependency," and "reciprocity" are used as the distinct,
separately-measured variables the course defines them as, not as
interchangeable synonyms for "closeness." Writing (20%) covers clarity and
structure across the three write-ups as a set.
