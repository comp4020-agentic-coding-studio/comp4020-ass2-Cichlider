## Before the session

List what your case's counterparty currently provides, then mark each item
fungible (a substitute could deliver it about as well) or specific (a
substitute couldn't, without rebuilding years of shared context).

## In the session

The room checks each other's fungible/specific splits for the classic
error: mistaking recency for specificity. A provision someone hasn't tested
a substitute for isn't automatically specific — it's just untested.

## Afterwards

This split, plus the reliability rate from week 3 and the five dependency
scores from week 4, is the raw material for the position paper due this
week. Bring all three to the writing.
