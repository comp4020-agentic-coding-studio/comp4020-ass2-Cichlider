## Before the session

Bring your week 1 paragraph and the front-end signal in it — the profile, the
opener, the first meeting, whatever counted as the initial price discovery.

## In the session

Pairs trade cases: each person tries to find the piece of information their
partner's case is quietly assuming the front-end signal proved. This is
where "they seemed really into it" usually gets downgraded to "they were
good at seeming into it," which is not an insult, just a variable
correction.

## Afterwards

Update your case paragraph with the corrected read. If the correction
changes what you think the situation actually is, say so — that revision is
itself evidence the model does something.
