## Before the session

Draft one paragraph naming which of the three diagnoses fits your case and
what evidence rules out the other two.

## In the session

Pairs stress-test each other's memo: the reader's job is to find the reading
of the same evidence that would support a different diagnosis. If they
can't, the memo holds. If they can, the case needs more evidence, not a
firmer opinion.

## Afterwards

Keep the memo. Week 8's case studies draw directly on gates that were
misdiagnosed this way, so a clean memo now saves rework later.
