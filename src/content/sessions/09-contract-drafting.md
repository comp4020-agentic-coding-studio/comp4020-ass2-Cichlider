## Before the session

For your case, name the contract type each party's behaviour implies —
separately for each party, not as a single shared answer.

## In the session

Groups look for the mismatch case: two parties behaving under different
contract types without having said so. When there's no mismatch, the group
instead checks whether the named contract type actually matches the
dependency and specificity levels measured back in weeks 4 and 5.

## Afterwards

This is the last case-review skill before the case study portfolio is due —
bring your contract-type findings across every case you're including in the
portfolio, not just your primary one.
