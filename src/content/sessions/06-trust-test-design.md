## Before the session

List the last three small trust tests in your case (a favour, a disclosure,
a small plan) and name who proposed each.

## In the session

Groups tally proposer counts across everyone's cases and look at the
distribution. A case where one party has proposed zero of the last several
tests is treated as a finding worth writing down, not an insult to that
party's character.

## Afterwards

Note the proposer split in your running case file — the case study
portfolio due in week 9 asks for this pattern across a set of cases, not
just one.
