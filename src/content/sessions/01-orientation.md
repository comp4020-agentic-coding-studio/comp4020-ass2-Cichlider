## Before the session

Bring the two-sentence situation and the observable-success statement above.
Composites are fine and, for anything involving a third party's private
details, preferred — the model doesn't need a true story, it needs a
plausible one.

## In the session

The room sorts submitted situations into "has a position" and "does not have
a position yet," then works through why a few of the second group are stuck.
Most of the stuck cases turn out to be feelings statements wearing a
situation's clothes.

## Afterwards

Each person leaves with a one-paragraph statement of their case rewritten in
two-party terms. That paragraph is the thing every later case review builds
on, so keep it.
