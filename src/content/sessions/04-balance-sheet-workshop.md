## Before the session

Score your case low/medium/high on time, emotional disclosure, social
integration, logistical entanglement, and identity dependency — five scores,
not one.

## In the session

Groups compare scoresheets and look for the case where one line item is
wildly out of step with the other four. That mismatch is usually the most
interesting thing about the case, and the thing the case owner had not
separately named before this week.

## Afterwards

Keep all five scores in your running notes. The position paper due in week 5
asks for exactly this breakdown, so this workshop is most of that
assignment's first section already done.
