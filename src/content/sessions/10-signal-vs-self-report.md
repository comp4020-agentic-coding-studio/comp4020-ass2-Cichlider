## Before the session

For your case, estimate the three signals over the last month: who
initiates plans, who discloses first, how fast conflicts get addressed once
raised.

## In the session

The room compares each person's signal trends against what they would have
said if simply asked "how's it going" — the gap between the two is the
point of the exercise, not a verdict on any individual case.

## Afterwards

Trends, not snapshots, matter here. Keep tracking these three signals weekly
through the rest of the course; the final audit in week 12 draws on a
trend, and a trend needs more than one data point.
