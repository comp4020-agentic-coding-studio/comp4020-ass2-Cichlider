## Before the session

For your case, estimate all seven signals over the last month: initiation
rate, disclosure rate, reciprocity balance, cost of the signal, specificity
of the signal, persistence, and repair speed.

## In the session

The room compares each person's signal trends against what they would have
said if simply asked "how's it going" — the gap between the two is the
point of the exercise, not a verdict on any individual case. Groups also
flag which of the seven signals is doing the most work for their case; it is
rarely the same one twice.

## Afterwards

Trends, not snapshots, matter here. Keep tracking all seven signals weekly
through the rest of the course; the final audit in week 12 draws on a trend,
and a trend needs more than one data point.
