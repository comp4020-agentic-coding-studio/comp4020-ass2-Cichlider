## Before the session

List the last five to ten planned interactions in your case and mark each
kept, rescheduled, or dropped. If your case doesn't have five interactions
yet, that's a finding too — say so.

## In the session

Convert each list to a rate and compare it against the adjective the case
owner started with. The gap between "they're flaky" and "3/7 kept" is
usually where the real disagreement in the room turns out to live.

## Afterwards

File the rate in your running case paragraph. Weeks 4 and 5 both build on
this number directly, so keep it computed, not remembered.
