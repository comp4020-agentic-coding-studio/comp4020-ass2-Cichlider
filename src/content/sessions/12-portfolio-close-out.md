## Before the session

Bring your case file with all ten variables' worth of notes gathered across
the semester — framing through risk.

## In the session

Each person walks their case through the full model out loud, in order. The
room's job is to catch skipped variables, not to relitigate ones already
settled in earlier reviews.

## Afterwards

This is the last case review before the final audit is due. There is no
homework beyond writing the audit itself — the case is finished; what's left
is stating the reading clearly.
