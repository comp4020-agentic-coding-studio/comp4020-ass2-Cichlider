## Before the session

Bring a stalled case — your own if it applies, or volunteer to work someone
else's with their consent.

## In the session

Clinic format: each case gets classified against the three failure modes in
turn, out loud, with the room pushing back on any classification that
doesn't cite specific evidence. The fix follows from the classification, not
from general advice.

## Afterwards

Classified cases feed directly into the case study portfolio due next week —
you'll need at least one worked example of each failure mode, so trade
notes with classmates whose case landed on a mode yours didn't.
