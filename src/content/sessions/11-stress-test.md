## Before the session

Draft a stop-loss condition for your case, specific enough that someone else
could check whether it had triggered without asking you.

## In the session

Pairs stress-test each other's stop-loss: is it actually checkable, or does
it quietly depend on the same judgement it's meant to route around? Then
each drafts one Black Swan Protocol step — who to talk to, what to check,
what not to decide alone.

## Afterwards

Keep both. The final audit in week 12 asks you to apply the full model to a
case, and a stop-loss with no evidence it was ever considered is one of the
more common gaps markers flag.
