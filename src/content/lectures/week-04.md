Once interaction is reliable enough to plan around, a party starts building
positions against it — time not spent elsewhere, routines built around
availability, social capital spent introducing the other person, plans made
that assume they'll still be there. That's dependency, and it is a balance
sheet with (at least) five line items: time, emotional disclosure, social
integration, logistical entanglement, and identity — how much of "who I am"
now routes through this relationship existing.

The mistake this week targets is collapsing all five into one number,
usually "how much I like them," and then being surprised when the balance
sheet doesn't move the way the feeling does. A person can be moderately
attracted and severely dependent — most of the entanglement of a bad
situationship is exactly that combination.

## Outline

- the five dependency line items, priced separately, not summed into one
