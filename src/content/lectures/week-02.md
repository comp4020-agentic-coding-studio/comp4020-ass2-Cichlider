Attraction is the front-end: the part of the system a counterparty sees
before either side has disclosed anything costly to fake. Front-ends are
cheap to signal and expensive to verify, which is exactly why so much early
dating behaviour is theatre — a curated profile, a rehearsed opener, a
venue chosen for effect. None of that is dishonest. It's marketing, and
marketing is supposed to be flattering.

The model's job here is narrow: separate the front-end signal from the
information it's standing in for. A profile photo prices "effort put into
self-presentation," not "compatibility." Confusing the two is the single
most common first-date mispricing, and it's the reason first impressions
correlate so weakly with everything that follows.
