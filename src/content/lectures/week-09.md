Once a commitment gate closes, the parties need a contract, and the model
names four: *Date* (recurring, non-exclusive, low mutual obligation),
*Exclusive* (recurring, closed to new positions, obligation still mostly
informal), *Official* (exclusive plus externally declared — the contract now
has third-party witnesses), and *Investment* (exclusive, declared, and
structurally entangled — shared logistics, shared social graph, shared
plans that assume duration). Each is a legitimate destination. The design
error this week targets is picking one contract type while behaving as if
you'd picked another — treating a Date-type arrangement with Investment-type
expectations is the single most common source of the "we were never on the
same page" complaint, and it's fully preventable by naming the contract type
out loud before assuming it.
