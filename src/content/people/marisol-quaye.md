Marisol built the course's model out of a long-running frustration with
relationship advice that treats every breakup as a unique tragedy rather than
a predictable failure of due diligence. Before Slop University she spent nine
years in behavioural economics, most of it modelling why people misprice risk
they can see coming. The relationship model is the same toolkit pointed at a
different market.

She teaches the lecture stream and holds the pen on the model itself — if a
case review produces a result the model doesn't explain cleanly, that's the
thing she wants to hear about first, not last.

Consultation hours are posted on the course calendar. Bring a specific
question, not a general one; "is my situationship a bad position" is a case
review, "does the commitment gate apply retroactively" is an email.
